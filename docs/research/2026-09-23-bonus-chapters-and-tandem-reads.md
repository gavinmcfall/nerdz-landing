# Bonus Chapters & Tandem Reads Research

Research for extending `/reading` with bonus-chapter placements for Throne of Glass (TOG), A Court of Thorns and Roses (ACOTAR), Crescent City (CC) and the Solaria universe, and for deciding the shape of a dedicated Solaria section (possibly with maps and art).

*Research date: 2026-09-23. Gathered by five parallel web-research agents; Fandom wikis for all four universes returned HTTP 402 and were only seen via search snippets, so wiki-sourced claims are marked as such.*

## Context

- Existing guides on `/reading`: EOS/ToD chapter tandem, Skyward, Cosmere, IAD, Breeds, Dark-Hunter, Iron Fey, Solaria (book-level). No ACOTAR, full-TOG or CC guides exist yet.
- Nearly all chapter anchors for bonus content come from **fan consensus**, not authors or publishers. Confidence below reflects how many independent sources agree.
- "Bonus" here means text outside the standard edition: retailer/special-edition exclusives, blog-tour pieces, charity anthologies, group freebies, companion novellas.

## Cross-universe (Maasverse) dependencies

- End of HOSB drops Bryce into Prythian; HOFAS spends roughly its first third there and relies on *A Court of Silver Flames* plot (Dread Trove, the Prison, post-ACOSF Nesta). **ACOSF must precede HOFAS**; the safest rule found is all five ACOTAR books before HOSB.
- The Walmart and BAM/Indigo HOFAS bonus chapters carry the same ACOSF dependency.
- TOG ↔ CC links (Brannon, Erilea-like Fae, Theia) are easter eggs; no TOG plot spoiler inside CC was documented. No direct TOG ↔ ACOTAR crossover found.

### Structure and crossovers

- Timeline per the authors' sites: Ruthless Boys ~5 years before ZA; Darkmore ~5 years after ZA. This rules out chapter-level interleaving of those series with ZA.
- Gabriel Nox (Ruthless Boys) is revealed as the twins' half-brother in ZA5, so ZA5 touches Ruthless Boys' outcome (partially inferred).
- Darkmore's Rosalie Oscura and Roary Night appear in Ruthless Boys; Roary is Leon's brother.
- Sins of the Zodiac: guides say "no crossover"; an April 2026 piece reports "hints of crossover". Books: Never Keep, Echo Fort, Cinder Vale (Mar 31, 2026), Oracle Bay (expected 2027).

## Rights considerations for hosting (not legal advice)

- Reading orders and chapter titles are facts/short phrases, but an original selection or arrangement (a tandem order) can be protected; copying a guide wholesale is also an attribution question. > [copyright.gov Circular 33](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ33.pdf), [Feist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feist_Publications,_Inc._v._Rural_Telephone_Service_Co.)
- Reference works with short own-words summaries have been treated as legitimate; the Harry Potter Lexicon lost for copying more than necessary. > [Warner Bros v. RDR Books summary](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/summaries/warnerbros-rdrbooks-sdny2008.pdf)
- New Zealand has fair dealing (criticism, review, news; s.42 Copyright Act 1994), not general fair use. > [Copyright law of NZ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copyright_law_of_New_Zealand)
- Linking to a page is generally not infringement; knowingly linking to infringing copies (e.g. posted scans of edition-exclusive chapters, common in fan trackers) can be contributory. > [dmlp.org](https://www.dmlp.org/legal-guide/linking-copyrighted-materials)
