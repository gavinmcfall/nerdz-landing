## Built vs designed-not-built

This guide reproduces the **currently built** Phase 1 — image generation, end-to-end. The wider Lighthouse vision (LLM routing, the kids' assistant, Cortex MCP seam, family portal, voice) is designed but not built; the guide stays scoped to what you can actually stand up.
